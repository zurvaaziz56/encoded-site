Questions

Good to know

What do I need to send?

Answers to a 15-minute questionnaire and three to five pieces you have already published and are happy with. If you have an AI draft that went wrong, send that too.

Which AI tools does it work with?

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Microsoft Copilot. You tick the tools your team uses and the kit is set up for each. The checker works in any AI chat.

Do we need anyone technical?

No. Setup takes about 10 minutes and the guide walks you through each tool step by step.

What if a rule is not quite right?

Reply with the rule and what you would change. One round of fixes is included. The Calibrated Kit goes further and tunes the rules to the edits your team actually makes.

Is our content safe?

We only ask for published work. It is used to build your kit and deleted 30 days after delivery, unless you choose the Calibrated Kit.
