What gets checked

The rules your editors enforce. Checked every time.

These are the kinds of checks we set up. Yours are written from your own house style, so they catch what your editors catch.

House rules · exampleRun on every draft
  1. The count matches the headline's promise
  2. The body matches the brief and the headline
  3. Every brief point gets its own item
  4. The headline uses the expert's most specific credential
  5. House subheading labels, used exactly
  6. No placeholders like "TBD" in figures
  7. Spelling, currency and terms match the outlet
  8. No em dashes
  9. No AI filler phrases
  10. It opens on the point
