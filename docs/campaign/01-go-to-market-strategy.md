# Encoded go-to-market strategy

5 November 2026 · For the House Rules Kit ($99), Calibrated Kit ($249) and custom systems.

## The one-line pitch

**Your house style, built into your AI.** Drafts that follow your rules the first time, and a checker that catches the ones that don't.

## Who buys

| | Primary buyer | Also buys |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who | The person who rereads every AI draft at a PR agency of 2 to 15 people: account director, editorial lead, or the agency owner | In-house comms teams, freelance PR writers, small content agencies |
| What they feel | "I brought in AI to save time and now I'm its proofreader." | "My clients each have a different style and I can't keep them straight." |
| Trigger moments | A client spots US spelling. A journalist bins a pitch. A "5 tips" piece goes out with 6. A new junior joins. | Taking on a new client with a fussy style guide |
| Where they are | LinkedIn (daily), PR trade newsletters, PR Facebook groups, Reddit r/PublicRelations and agency-owner subs, PR Slack/Discord groups, podcasts | Same, plus freelance writing communities |

## Why they'd pick Encoded

1. **It's about their rules, not "brand voice".** Voice tools learn a vibe. Encoded writes down checkable rules (counts, credentials, labels, spelling) and catches every break.
2. **$99, no subscription, no new tool.** Works inside the AI they already pay for.
3. **A person builds it.** Zurva checks every kit. That matters to PR people, who trust editors more than software.
4. **Proof before purchase.** A free sample kit and a test report show exactly what arrives.

## The message ladder (use these words everywhere)

- **Problem:** "AI drafts still need rewriting."
- **Villain:** the rewrite pass (never the writers, never AI itself).
- **Promise:** "Your house style, built into your AI."
- **Proof:** the sample kit; the before-and-after; the live systems (8-stage QA pipeline, 200+ podcast episodes, a daily LinkedIn engine).
- **Price:** "$99. Ready in 2 business days."
- **Next step:** "Get your kit" or "Download the free sample".

## Channels, ranked for a solo seller with a $99 product

1. **LinkedIn, Zurva's personal profile (main engine).** PR buyers live here, and people buy from people. 3 posts a week, 20 minutes of comments a day, 10 personal DMs a week. See `linkedin.md`.
2. **Direct outreach to small PR agencies.** Fastest path to the first 10 customers. Personal note plus the free sample kit, never a hard sell. Scripts in `linkedin.md` and `tiktok-reddit-more.md`.
3. **Reddit (value first).** r/PublicRelations and agency and AI-writing communities: answer "how do I make ChatGPT follow our style guide?" threads properly. Mention the kit only where rules allow. See `tiktok-reddit-more.md`.
4. **Instagram and TikTok (brand and reach).** PR-life humour and before-and-afters travel well; they build the brand and feed LinkedIn. See `instagram-facebook.md` and `tiktok-reddit-more.md`.
5. **Facebook groups.** Small agency owner and PR freelancer groups: helpful posts, the free checklist, no spam.
6. **Borrowed audiences.** Guest pieces in PR newsletters, PR podcasts, partnerships with PR course creators and freelance PR networks.

## Lead magnets (free things that start the conversation)

- **The free sample kit** (already on the site): the full kit for a made-up agency.
- **"The 12 rules AI breaks in press releases"**: a one-page checklist (turn the rules library into a PDF).
- **The 2-minute test**: paste this checker prompt and a draft into any AI and see what breaks.

## Weekly rhythm (about 5 hours a week)

- **Monday:** 45 minutes. Schedule the week's LinkedIn and Instagram posts.
- **Every weekday:** 20 minutes. Comments on LinkedIn, one Reddit answer.
- **Tuesday and Thursday:** 30 minutes. 5 personal DMs each day.
- **Wednesday:** 45 minutes. Film 2 to 3 short videos in one go.
- **Friday:** 15 minutes. Red Pen Friday, and log the week's numbers.

## Targets to aim for (goals, not predictions)

| By the end of week 6 | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| Sample kit downloads | 100 |
| Conversations with agencies (DM replies or calls) | 30 |
| Kits sold | 10 |
| Calibrated upgrades | 2 |
| Custom-system calls | 2 |

If after week 4 there are downloads but no orders, the price or page is the problem: talk to 5 downloaders. If there are no downloads, the message is the problem: change the hooks, not the product.

## Budget

- **$0 version:** everything above is organic.
- **Small paid test (optional):** $5 to $10 a day for 2 weeks on Instagram and Facebook, promoting the best-performing carousel to PR job titles, sending people to the free sample kit. Stop if a download costs more than $5.

## Guardrails

- Never name real clients or invent results, quotes or numbers. Use the made-up Fernhill PR for demos.
- Never call Zurva a "founder". She is a copywriter and content strategist who builds editorial AI systems.
- No em dashes, no hype words, no fake urgency. The brand voice is the product: if the posts sound like AI slop, nobody will trust the kit.
